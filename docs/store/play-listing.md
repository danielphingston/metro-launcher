# Play listing

## Short description (80 max)

Live tiles, smart search and news on your home screen. Barely touches battery.

## Full description (4000 max)

Metro Launcher turns your home screen into a grid of live tiles that show what matters at a glance: messages, music, weather, calendar, news, your next ride and the time, without opening a single app.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Small, medium, wide and large tiles you arrange freely, with undo for every change
• Customise any tile from one sheet: name, icon, icon pack glyph, your own photo, colour and transparency
• Icon packs (ADW, Nova, Apex) and themed icons
• 20 accent colours, light or dark, solid background, photo tiles, full photo or your wallpaper
• Folders that open right under the tile, with their own styles
• A short first-run setup gets you going in a minute
• Works on phones, tablets and foldables

LIVE TILES
• Weather with a flip side for the forecast
• Music with album art and controls
• Messages and unread counts on app tiles
• Me tile: every app's newest notification, flipping through them one by one
• Calendar with your next event
• People, photos, clock and more
• Pin widgets and shortcuts from any app, resize widgets right on start

SMART SEARCH
Pull down and type. Metro Launcher finds apps even with typos or initials, and answers right in the list:
• Calculator and unit conversions
• "timer 5 min", "alarm 7:30", "note buy milk"
• Phone settings, contacts, calendar events and chats
• Search inside apps such as YouTube, Play Store, Maps and Amazon
• Your own short keywords: type "yt cats" to search YouTube
• Ask your AI assistant app straight from search
• Pin any result to start

APP LIST
• Alphabetical list with a jump grid and a "recently added" group
• Alphabet scrubber with a wave effect on the edge

FAST AND LIGHT
• Smooth turnstile, slide, zoom or fade animations, with adjustable speed
• Barely touches your battery: tiles update only when something changes, and nothing runs in the background to refresh news
• Back up and restore your tiles and settings to a file or Google Drive
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking. What you see on your home screen stays on your phone.

METRO PLUS
An optional one-time purchase unlocks:
• Feed tile: headlines with pictures from Lemmy communities and RSS or Atom feeds, sliding in one by one like Windows Phone news. Several feeds per tile, refreshed only when you look at start
• Tracking tile: your ride, food order or parcel with its arrival time, progress and OTP, read from the app's own notification on your phone. Tap to open the app's tracking screen
• Layouts: several start screens, such as work and weekend, each with its own tiles and wallpaper. Switch with a tile, from search, or from automation apps
• Gestures: double tap, two-finger swipe and pinch on the start screen
• Smart search, search in apps, keywords and ask assistant
• Assistant tile with your assistant's latest reply
• Quick settings tiles: flashlight, do not disturb, ringer, auto-rotate, brightness and battery

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE (OPTIONAL)
Metro Launcher includes an optional accessibility service, "Metro gestures", used for one thing: when you set up a start screen gesture (double tap, two-finger swipe or pinch) to turn the screen off, open recent apps, the notification shade or quick settings, Metro uses Android's Accessibility API to perform that action. The service is off by default, and Metro explains it and asks for your consent in Settings → gestures before you turn it on. It does not read or collect anything on your screen, what you type or which apps you use, and nothing is stored or shared. Without it, every other feature works; only those gestures do nothing.

The feed tile downloads only the feeds you add, straight from those sites. Notifications for the me and tracking tiles are read on your phone and never leave it.
